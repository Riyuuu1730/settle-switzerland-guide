# Settle: The Story Behind the Product

**From a real world problem to an AI assisted prototype.**

## The idea

Moving to Switzerland was exciting, but settling into a new country also meant navigating unfamiliar administrative processes, housing rules, language barriers, and everyday decisions.

I saw an opportunity to make that experience less overwhelming.

Settle started with a simple question: what if newcomers could find the guidance they need during their first weeks in Switzerland in one accessible place?

## The problem I wanted to solve

Information about settling into Switzerland is spread across different websites, local authorities, and service providers. For someone unfamiliar with the language or local processes, knowing what to do, when to do it, and where to find reliable information can be difficult.

The opportunity was not simply to create another information website. It was to make the experience more practical, approachable, and easier to navigate.

## My product thinking

Before building anything, I worked through the product questions that would shape the experience.

**Who is the user?**

People who have recently moved to Switzerland and need practical guidance as they settle into a new environment.

**What is their immediate need?**

A clear starting point, an understanding of essential tasks, and help navigating unfamiliar situations.

**What should the product prioritise?**

The first 30 days, housing and local rules, everyday essentials, official letters, and contextual assistance.

**How should the experience feel?**

Simple, friendly, and accessible, with clear actions rather than overwhelming users with information.

These decisions shaped the initial product concept and its core features.

## From concept to prototype

I used Lovable and AI assisted development to turn the concept into a working, interactive prototype.

My focus was on defining the problem, shaping the product experience, selecting the initial features, and translating those decisions into a tangible application.

The prototype brings together a personalised onboarding checklist, housing guidance, daily life information, an AI powered Letter Helper, and Ask Buddy for contextual assistance.

The goal was to move beyond an idea and create something people could explore and respond to.

## Taking the idea beyond the screen

I presented Settle during a digital transformation session in Geneva, where participants explored the idea and shared positive feedback.

That experience reinforced the value of making an idea tangible. Instead of explaining a concept in a presentation alone, I could demonstrate the proposed experience and invite people to react to it.

I also shared the prototype on LinkedIn to invite further feedback on the idea, user experience, and design.

## What this project represents

With five years of professional experience, I see this project as an opportunity to bring my product mindset into hands on AI assisted development.

It reflects how I approach product work: understand the problem, think through the user journey, prioritise what matters, and use technology to explore solutions faster.

It also highlights an important distinction. Building a prototype is a starting point, not proof of product market fit. The next stage is to validate the assumptions with target users and use evidence to guide improvements.

## What comes next

The next steps are to gather structured feedback from people who have recently moved to Switzerland, understand which features deliver the most value, and prioritise improvements based on their needs.

Further opportunities include expanding local guidance, improving the experience of interpreting official letters, and exploring how newcomers could discover relevant communities and events.
